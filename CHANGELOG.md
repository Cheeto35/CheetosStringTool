# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Build strings from a searchable list of every item, with real names for items, slots and materials.
- Parts list showing every slot and what's in it, plus a 2D drawing of the build at real size, coloured like the game's materials.
- Materials for whole items and for single pieces of crafted items (lantern bars, pouch flaps, quiver straps).
- Dock contents, like 999 arrows in a quiver.
- Attach a whole string to a slot or dock.
- Gift boxes: To and From names, and anything packed inside, including whole strings and other gift boxes.
- Item settings: potion liquids and custom mixes, dynamite fuses, durability, never despawning, cooked food, firework colours, enchantments, stats and every other value the game saves.
- Spawn preview and Clear preview on the connected server.
- Copy and paste strings, and save your own next to almost 200 of Cheeto's strings.
- Reads the connected server's own item list, so modded items work too.
